YouTube Downloader for Mac
==========================

1. Drag "YouTube Downloader.app" into your Applications folder.

2. Open it.  macOS will say it "cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software"
   (this app is not signed with a paid Apple developer certificate).  Do ONE of these:

   macOS 15 (Sequoia) and newer:
     Click "Done", then open  System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway"
     next to YouTube Downloader, and confirm with your password / Touch ID.

   macOS 14 and older:
     Control-click (right-click) the app > Open > Open.

   Or, in Terminal (works on every version):
     xattr -cr "/Applications/YouTube Downloader.app"

3. The first start downloads two free helpers (yt-dlp and ffmpeg, about 100 MB). A banner shows the progress.
   Then your browser opens the app. A download-arrow icon appears in the menu bar (top right).

Using it
  - Paste YouTube links, tick MP3 and/or MP4, click "Add to queue".
  - Closing the browser tab does NOT stop downloads. Menu bar icon > Quit to stop everything.
  - Settings tab: limit downloads, pause, choose the save folder, "Start when I log in",
    and "Let phones and tablets on my Wi-Fi use this" (shows an address and PIN for your iPhone / Android).

Only download audio and video you have the right to.
Full guide: docs/MACOS.md in the project (https://github.com/HDumo/YoutubeDownloader).
